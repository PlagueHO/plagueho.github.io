**Marginalia** helps long-form writers refine manuscripts while preserving their voice. Writers can upload a Word document or paste a draft, guide the analysis with tone and custom instructions, review AI-generated suggestions, and export the accepted revisions as a polished document.

The application uses Azure AI Foundry for paragraph-level editorial analysis and Microsoft Aspire to orchestrate its React frontend, .NET backend, and local development services.
